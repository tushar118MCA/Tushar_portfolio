About Me

I'm a data analyst driven by curiosity — I don't just look at numbers, I dig for the story behind them. With a Master of Computer Applications (MCA) background, I've spent the past one year building hands-on projects that transform raw, messy datasets into clear, actionable insights, using tools and techniques to clean, analyze, and visualize data in ways that make sense to real decision-makers. What excites me most about this field is the problem-solving process: taking a vague, open-ended business question, figuring out exactly what data will actually answer it, and then communicating the findings in a way non-technical stakeholders can immediately understand and act on. I enjoy the entire journey — from exploring raw data to uncovering patterns to presenting a clear recommendation. I'm now seeking an entry-level data analyst role where I can apply these skills to real business problems, contribute meaningfully to a team, and grow into a specialist in data-driven decision-making.

My Recent Works

A selection of projects I've built to turn raw data into actionable insights and dashboards.


HR Analytics Dashboard

Problem

An organization had 1,000 raw employee records but no way to track headcount, compensation, or diversity trends across departments and business units.

Approach & Result

Cleaned and engineered features in Python, modeled the data in PostgreSQL with SQL window functions and reusable views, and built a 3-page Power BI dashboard. Uncovered a mislabeled salary-band bucket misclassifying the highest earners as "Very Low."

Languages and Tools

Python SQL DAX Power BI

Telecommunication Financial Dashboard

Problem

Indian telecom companies had years of raw XBRL P&L filings in a database, with no easy way to compare profitability or spot which companies were losing money.

Approach & Result

Built a Python ETL pipeline to extract sector-specific filings, wrote SQL to derive EBITDA, EBIT, PBT, and PAT margins, and built a Power BI dashboard. Found an overall PAT margin of -10.99% despite EBITDA of 20.74T, with one company driving about 59% of total sector EBITDA.

Languages and Tools

Python SQL DAX Power BI

Zomato Analytics Dashboard

Problem

Zomato's raw export had 51K+ messy restaurant listings across 30 cities, with no way to track pricing trends, rating patterns, or underperforming cities and cuisines.

Approach & Result

Cleaned and modeled 51K+ rows in Python, built a PostgreSQL backend with SQL views, and designed a Power BI dashboard with rating and cost KPIs. Found that online-order-enabled outlets averaged meaningfully higher ratings than offline-only outlets.

Languages and Tools

Python SQL Power BI DAX

Sales Analytics Project

Problem

Sales data across products, customers, and regions was scattered across raw transaction records, making it hard to spot revenue trends or flag at-risk customer segments.

Approach & Result

Built a Power BI dashboard on 12,400+ orders with KPI tracking, trend analysis, and demographic breakdowns. Surfaced a 49.6% churn rate and revenue concentration in just two city and two product segments.

Languages and Tools

Python SQL DAX Power BI

Hospital Performance & Patient Outcome Analytics Project

Problem

A hospital had 50K+ raw patient records scattered with duplicates, missing values, and corrupted date fields, with no reliable way to track outcomes, costs, or doctor performance.

Approach & Result

Cleaned and modeled 18K+ visits in Python into a 3-table relational schema for patients, doctors, and visits, then wrote validated SQL queries for KPIs. Found a 7.5% readmission rate and 18% cost variance across departments.

Languages and Tools

Python SQL DAX Power BI
